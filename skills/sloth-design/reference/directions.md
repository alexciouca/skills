# Directions

A local catalog of visual directions for `new`. Each is a starting point to
adapt to the product, not a template to copy. Type is given as a kind of
face, not a named font: use what the project hosts or what fits its license.

Fields: **Type**, **Color**, **Layout**, **Signature** (the one detail that
makes it recognisable), **Fits** (modes), **Avoid when**.

### Swiss Timetable
- Type: one neutral grotesk in three weights; large numerals.
- Color: white or off-white, black, one signal red.
- Layout: strict column grid, flush left, rules between rows.
- Signature: data or times set big as the main visual.
- Fits: Work, Read, Sell. Avoid when: the brand is soft or playful.

### Control Room
- Type: compact sans for labels, mono for every number.
- Color: deep slate, muted panels, amber and teal for status only.
- Layout: dense tiled panels, fixed header, everything visible at once.
- Signature: live status lights and tabular readouts.
- Fits: Work. Avoid when: users are casual or first-time.

### Broadsheet
- Type: condensed serif headlines, readable serif body, small sans captions.
- Color: newsprint gray-white, ink black, one muted accent.
- Layout: multi-column front page, hairline rules, a dominant lead story.
- Signature: datelines, bylines and section flags.
- Fits: Read, Sell. Avoid when: there is little text content.

### Workshop Manual
- Type: sturdy sans, numbered step headings, mono for part numbers.
- Color: light gray, black linework, safety yellow for warnings.
- Layout: numbered steps beside diagrams, callouts with leader lines.
- Signature: exploded diagrams and step numbers in circles.
- Fits: Read (docs, onboarding), Work. Avoid when: the product is luxury.

### Blueprint
- Type: technical mono or narrow grotesk, all-caps labels.
- Color: deep blue ground, pale cyan lines, white text.
- Layout: faint measurement grid, annotated diagrams, dimension lines.
- Signature: drawings with measurements and leader notes.
- Fits: Sell (developer and hardware tools), Show. Avoid when: long reading.

### Lab Report
- Type: readable serif or humanist sans, mono for values.
- Color: white, black, one figure color, muted gray for gridlines.
- Layout: figures with numbered captions, footnotes in the margin.
- Signature: "Fig. 3" captions and real data charts up front.
- Fits: Read, Work (analytics). Avoid when: there is no real data to show.

### Ledger
- Type: book serif headings, sans body, tabular numerals everywhere.
- Color: warm paper, dark green ink, red only for negatives.
- Layout: aligned columns, double rules for totals, wide margins.
- Signature: accounting-style totals and right-aligned figures.
- Fits: Work (finance, billing). Avoid when: the audience is young and casual.

### Transit Signage
- Type: bold, wide, highly legible sans; pictograms.
- Color: dark or white ground, one color per category or line.
- Layout: big wayfinding labels, color-coded bands, arrows.
- Signature: route-line diagrams and colored category dots.
- Fits: Work (navigation, booking), Read (help centers). Avoid when: the
  content has no categories.

### Clinic
- Type: large, open humanist sans; generous size and spacing.
- Color: soft white, deep ink, one calm accent, clear status colors.
- Layout: one task per screen, big targets, plain steps.
- Signature: reassuring plain-language copy and visible progress.
- Fits: Work (health, government, forms). Avoid when: it must feel exciting.

### Instrument
- Type: precise narrow sans, small caps labels, mono values.
- Color: light gray metal tones, black, one indicator color.
- Layout: aligned gauges and readouts, hairline dividers.
- Signature: hairline dials, scales and tick marks.
- Fits: Work (monitoring, IoT), Sell (hardware). Avoid when: text-heavy.

### Concert Poster
- Type: huge condensed or heavy display type that crops off the edges.
- Color: one saturated color plus black or white.
- Layout: stacked type as the image, dates and details in a tight block.
- Signature: words larger than the screen.
- Fits: Sell (events, launches), Show. Avoid when: Work screens or long copy.

### Risograph
- Type: chunky grotesk or friendly serif.
- Color: two or three spot inks (pink, blue, yellow) that overlap.
- Layout: loose collage, offset color layers, grain texture.
- Signature: overprinted shapes and slight misregistration.
- Fits: Sell (indie, culture, education), Show. Avoid when: corporate or
  high-trust finance.

### Zine
- Type: mixed faces, handwritten notes, typewriter captions.
- Color: photocopy black and white plus one marker color.
- Layout: cut-and-paste collage, rotated elements, tape and staples.
- Signature: hand-made marks on a digital page.
- Fits: Show, Sell (community, music). Avoid when: users need speed.

### Museum Label
- Type: small, precise sans or serif; restrained sizes.
- Color: gallery white or warm gray, black text.
- Layout: very large work, wide empty margins, captions like wall labels.
- Signature: title, year and medium-style captions.
- Fits: Show, Sell (premium). Avoid when: the content is dense or small.

### Gallery Wall
- Type: one quiet sans, captions only.
- Color: neutral ground that disappears behind the work.
- Layout: mixed-size grid following each piece's shape, no frames.
- Signature: the hang itself: rhythm of sizes and gaps.
- Fits: Show. Avoid when: the items are not visual.

### Letterpress
- Type: heavy old-style serif, ornaments used sparingly.
- Color: off-white textured paper, deep ink, one second ink.
- Layout: centered, formal composition, rules and borders.
- Signature: printed-feel type with slight ink weight.
- Fits: Sell (crafts, food, weddings), Show. Avoid when: tech or Work UIs.

### Botanical
- Type: light elegant serif headings, quiet sans body.
- Color: muted greens and earth, lots of off-white.
- Layout: large photography, asymmetric text blocks, generous margins.
- Signature: specimen-style plates with Latin-like labels.
- Fits: Sell (lifestyle, wellness), Show. Avoid when: the product is digital
  infrastructure.

### Field Notes
- Type: typewriter or mono notes, serif headings, hand-drawn marks.
- Color: graph-paper grid, pencil gray, one ink color.
- Layout: notebook pages, numbered entries, sketches beside text.
- Signature: annotated sketches and dated entries.
- Fits: Read (blogs, research), Show. Avoid when: polish must feel corporate.

### Atlas
- Type: classic map serif for places, small sans for data.
- Color: muted earth and water tones, contour lines.
- Layout: map or contour textures, coordinates, layered legends.
- Signature: a legend box and coordinates as decoration with meaning.
- Fits: Sell, Read (data, travel, geo). Avoid when: nothing is spatial.

### Postcard
- Type: bold sans or script accent, used sparingly.
- Color: taken from one strong photo.
- Layout: one full-bleed image, a short message, a stamp-like badge.
- Signature: the stamp and postmark details.
- Fits: Sell (travel, hospitality). Avoid when: no strong photo exists.

### Night Market
- Type: hand-painted-sign style display, simple sans body.
- Color: deep plum or warm black, lantern red, gold, green.
- Layout: dense stalls of offers, signs hung at different heights.
- Signature: glowing sign headings over a dark ground.
- Fits: Sell (food, events). Avoid when: calm or trust is the goal.

### Arcade
- Type: chunky rounded or pixel display, simple body.
- Color: saturated primaries on dark, high contrast.
- Layout: big buttons, score-like numbers, playful asymmetry.
- Signature: game-like feedback on every action.
- Fits: Sell (games, kids, learning), Show. Avoid when: serious or
  professional tools.

### Plain Index
- Type: the system font stack, default sizes barely changed.
- Color: white, black, blue underlined links.
- Layout: a single readable column, visible structure, no decoration.
- Signature: honesty: it looks exactly like what it is, and loads instantly.
- Fits: Read, Show (personal sites). Avoid when: the brand needs warmth.

### Paper Terminal
- Type: mono throughout, one weight plus bold.
- Color: warm gray paper, near-black text, one highlight color.
- Layout: command-line rhythm, keyboard hints, compact lists.
- Signature: visible shortcuts and prompt-style headings.
- Fits: Work (developer tools), Read (CLI docs). Avoid when: non-technical
  users.
